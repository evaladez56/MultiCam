How to Use

  1. Setup — Choose the number of cameras, then click Initialize Cameras. Select which camera and audio inputs to use.
    • Individual Cameras — Check Record Separately on any camera to also save its solo feed.
    • Timer & Split Timers — Enable the timer overlay to burn elapsed time into the video. Use Machine/Manual time buttons to track split durations.
  2. Record — Click Start Recording. All camera feeds are combined into a single grid video. Use Pause and Resume as needed.
  3. Stop & Save — Click Stop Recording. Your file downloads automatically.
